Common questions

How do I export my projects?

Open a project, choose Share, then Export. You can export as CSV or PDF.

Can I invite people outside my team?

Yes. Guests can view and comment on the projects you share with them.

How do I change my email address?

Go to Settings, then Account. We send a confirmation link to the new address.
